# Paper Shot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Paper Shot is a memory game published by happylife2080 / kirin Labs (kwan765).

## Data used by the game

Paper Shot does not require an account. The game does not collect or transmit personal information, use advertising, or include third-party analytics SDKs.

Your best score, highest round, tutorial status, and preferences (music, sound, vibration, screen shake, and reduced motion) are saved locally on your device or in your browser's local storage. These values are not sent to the developer.

## Platforms

Google Play, Toss, and itch.io process information needed to provide their own platform services under their respective privacy policies. This policy describes the Paper Shot game itself.

## Deleting local data

Clear the game's app data or uninstall the Android app to remove local progress and preferences. For the browser version, clear the site's stored data in your browser settings. Platform backups may be governed by your device or platform settings.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change. For questions about privacy or the game, contact **kwan765@naver.com**.
